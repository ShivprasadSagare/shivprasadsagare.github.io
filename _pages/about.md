Hello there👋 I hope you're having a good day🙂 Welcome to my digital space🙏 

I’m an Applied Scientist 2 at Microsoft in Hyderabad, India. I work on AI systems for query suggestions across Bing and Copilot—focusing on LLM post-training, fast inference, real-time ranking, and automated evaluation.

Previously, as a Machine Learning Scientist at PhroneticAI in Bengaluru, I worked on multimodal LLMs for video analytics, integrating audio🔊, visual🎥, and textual signals📝, along with long-video retrieval (VideoRAG). Before that, as an Applied Research Scientist at NeuralSpace, I trained and deployed Speech-to-Text🗣️ systems.

Earlier, I completed my Master's by Research at the Language Technologies Research Centre (LTRC), IIIT Hyderabad 🎓 My thesis work revolved around cross-lingual text generation in low-resource (especially Indian) languages.

Here, I share my learnings as a researcher and developer💻, plus some stories of my journey🌏. Apart from work, I am into novels📚, music🎹, and fitness🧘🏻‍♂️. I also like hiking🏔️, exploring cities🚏, and trying good coffees☕.
